# Program explainer videos — scripts (one per program)

Prepared on 2026-11-17 at the student's request: one video per program, for recruitment.

**The idea.** A visitor asks the robot about a program. The robot's smiling face gives way to an "explanation board" (title, numbers, a quick look at the study plan, career fields, why study it here). At the end the face comes back.

**Facts.** Every number and claim below comes from **approved** knowledge: the official 2025/2026 study plans, the department pages, the Student Handbook 2025/2026, and the approved explainers and FAQs in `/admin`. Claims that come from the university's own wording are marked "حسب الجامعة". Nothing may be added to a video that is not in these sources (CLAUDE.md rule 1).

## Two versions per program
| Version | Length | Use | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Board video** (scenes 1–8) | about 85 s | played on the robot screen when a visitor asks for a full explanation of a program | the robot's own voice (same TTS voice as the robot), so it sounds like the robot talking. The microphone stays muted while it plays (normal turn-taking) |
| **B. Promo video** (scene 0 + scenes 1–8) | about 95 s | social media, open day, defense | same narration, plus the visitor dialogue |

**Honesty rules for production**
- AI-generated shots (people, labs, robots) are **illustrations**. Show a small "لقطة توضيحية" label, and never present them as the real MEU campus, labs or students.
- Do not use real people's faces, and do not use the MEU logo unless the university allows it.
- The robot in the promo is a **concept** until the real robot is built. The final demonstration video of the project must show the real robot.
- Check every number on screen against this script before uploading.

**Format.** Export MP4, 1920×1080 (it fits the 1024×600 screen), 50 MB or less. Upload it in `/admin → Media`, link it to the program, approve it.

**Style.** The robot's colours (dark red and white), large Arabic text written right-to-left, simple line icons, the same line drawing as the robot's explainer for each program, and calm background music under the voice (low volume).

## Production log — 2026-11-17

**HyperFrames was tried and dropped.** Two runs on the ISE video (project `f2677c43-…`):
- Run 1 (light theme): the title was tiny in a corner, the robot answered before the visitor asked, «بكالوريوس» overflowed its card, and the auto-captions said «مواد التخصص 32 ساعة» instead of 83.
- Run 2 (dark theme, slower voice): most card text stayed dark on the dark background (unreadable), the captions said «33» instead of 83, and the narration ended at 0:58 followed by 25 s of a still face.

HyperFrames captions are transcribed from its own voice, so numbers cannot be guaranteed, and each edit takes about 12 minutes and may use a monthly credit.

**What we use instead: our own renderer.**
- **Scenes:** HTML scenes rendered frame by frame with Playwright and encoded with ffmpeg (1920×1080, 30 fps). The look is dark navy with white and gold text, a red glow and line drawings that draw themselves.
- **Voice:** the robot's own voice, from the backend's `POST /api/tts` (gpt-4o-mini-tts, voice coral, the robot's Jordanian instructions), at natural speed. It is not the robot's 1.12 playback rate.
- **Captions:** written by hand from the script, so every number on screen is exactly the source number.
- **Robot face:** the small face in the corner talks in sync with the audio.
- **Code:** `engine.html`, `build.py`, `render.js` and one `spec.json` per program. It can be added to the repo under `tools/video/` after sprint 4 finishes.

Lengths: ISE 1:55 · Architecture 2:03 · Renewable Energy 2:02.

## How to produce one video (suggested)
1. **Board scenes 1–8:** motion graphics in **HyperFrames by HeyGen** (a Claude connector that builds animated slides and renders video), or Canva. Use the board texts above exactly.
2. **Background clips:** generate them with Higgsfield or another video model using the prompts above, labelled «لقطة توضيحية».
3. **Voice:** record the full narration with the robot's own voice. The backend can export it with the same TTS voice and instructions, so the video sounds like the robot. A small backend command for this can be added in the next sprint.
4. **Finish:** put it together, check every number, export MP4 (50 MB or less), upload to `/admin → Media`, link it to the program and approve it.
